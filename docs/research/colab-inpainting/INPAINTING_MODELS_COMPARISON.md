# Inpainting model comparison for Yomu

Status: literature/source comparison plus one synthetic T4 smoke of Stable Diffusion Inpainting. That smoke was blocked by its safety checker and is **not a quality validation or model winner**. “Quality”, speed, and per-page time remain `NOT_MEASURED` unless explicitly called out. Editing is not equivalent to binary-mask inpainting.

## Yomu baseline and gates

The existing production path already has two relevant approaches:

1. `yomu/clean/inpainting/art_text_inpainting.py` and `multiscale_patch_synthesis.py`: local, OpenCV-based, mask-constrained reconstruction with contamination contracts, donor exclusion, texture checks, residual detection, and explicit outside-mask difference reporting. No generative GPU is implied by this path.
2. `yomu/clean/inpainting/advanced_art_inpainting.py`: an optional TorchScript, mask-conditioned LaMa-family adapter. It loads an external checkpoint only after SHA-256 verification, crops to mask context, predicts, and copies prediction pixels only inside the binary mask. Its default device is CPU. The configured anime/manga checkpoint is 205,717,870 bytes and its descriptor explicitly says `NEEDS FUTURE REVIEW for public redistribution`; do not ship/re-distribute it until provenance/license review closes.

At the clean strategy boundary, only a sufficiently confident `LOCAL_RECONSTRUCTION_HARD` region with an authorized mask can route remotely. The generated/donor image is not final authority: Yomu composites against the original inside the authorized mask and checks `OUTSIDE_MASK_CHANGED_PIXELS=0`; failure is review, not acceptance. These contracts remain unchanged in this research.

## Preliminary recommendation (not a benchmark winner)

If checkpoint rights are cleared, benchmark the already-integrated anime/manga Big-LaMa adapter first: it is lightweight by checkpoint file size, consumes an explicit mask, and the Yomu adapter already enforces SHA integrity and outside-mask composition. That is a **test order**, not a quality or speed win. For hard regions, compare against one explicitly mask-conditioned generative candidate in a research-only setting; prompt editors such as Qwen Edit or FLUX.2 Klein must be labeled `EDITING_NOT_MASKED_INPAINT` and cannot bypass the authorized-mask composite.

No candidate has demonstrated best quality, best speed, preservation of faces/line art, or pages/minute on the same Yomu fixtures. The benchmark gate is open. Do not choose an 80 GB accelerator or a larger checkpoint until a paired test shows a measurable benefit over the existing small mask model/local path.
